Leads

Leads gives you an efficient way to find new work. When a customer posts a job, whether directly or through a hipages partner like IKEA or Bunnings, it shows up as a lead for you. No need to spend hours chasing new work, you choose the jobs you want. The same leads are available on both the hipages for Business app and web, so you can choose whichever platform suits you.

When you accept a lead, a corresponding job is automatically created in Jobs, where you can manage everything for that job, from quoting through to payment.

Note:

To receive and accept leads, you'll need a paid hipages for business subscription.

Credits

Accepting leads uses credits. Your subscription includes a monthly allowance based on your package, and you can top up any time with a lead credits pack if you run out. Credits must be used within the timeframe set by your subscription or credits pack. For more on topping up, see Managing your package and credit usage.

Navigation

hipages for business app

The Leads tab shows all your available leads, displayed in compact view by default. Compact view is the recommended way to browse leads, showing the key details plus an AI-generated job summary, giving you the essentials at a glance so you can decide if a job's right for you without reading the full description.

If you switch to expanded view, you'll see the full job details instead, so the AI summary isn't needed. If you can't see job summaries, see Changing your lead view to update your viewing options.

The Leads screen has three tabs to help you sort and review your leads:

  • All — shows all your available leads in one view

  • Premium leads — highlights leads with signals of high-value or high-intent work, such as renovation projects, new builds, or ongoing work

  • Today's deals — showcases unclaimed leads as special offers at a reduced credit rate, available for a limited time each day

You can also switch how leads are displayed between expanded, compact, and map view. For more, see Changing your lead view.

hipages for business web

The web shows your full list of available leads. There's currently no way to filter, sort, or change how leads are displayed, this is currently only available on the hipages for Business app.

You can accept or decline any lead that's available to you.

If a lead's already been accepted by three other businesses, you can join the waitlist to express your interest in the job. Joining the waitlist lets the customer know your business is available and interested in quoting, even if they already have three businesses lined up.
